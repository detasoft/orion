## Rules

- Do not maintain a separate root `TASKS.md` task list.
- Keep each `TASK.md` focused on status, scope, ownership, and immediate child
  tasks.
- Keep detailed designs and implementation steps in ordinary plan files under
  `docs/plans/`.
