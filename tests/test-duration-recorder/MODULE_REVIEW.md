# Module Review: `tests/test-duration-recorder`

## 3. Carriage returns in display names break CSV records

**Problem.** `csvValue` quotes commas, quotes and LF, but not CR. Parameters such as `/item\r` and `help\r`
produce display names with an unquoted carriage return inside the output record.

**Sources.** [CSV quoting and display-name output](src/main/java/pro/deta/orion/test/duration/TestAnalyticsReport.java),
[real producer](../../core/command/src/test/java/pro/deta/orion/command/terminal/InteractiveTerminalTest.java),
and [report tests](src/test/java/pro/deta/orion/test/duration/TestAnalyticsReportTest.java).

**Documented behavior and contract.** The [README](../../README.md) promises CSV artifacts, generated by
[Make](../../Makefile) after JFR runs. A display name, including CR, must remain inside one CSV record.

**Minimal repair.** Include CR in the quoting condition. Round-trip the generated CSV through a reader for
CR, LF, commas and quotes.

**Alternatives and consequences.** Quoting every field changes more output; replacing CR loses data.
No new production dependency is needed.

**Confidence.** High from code, current producers and recorded JSONL; generated CSV was not exercised in the audit.
**Priority signals.** Medium importance: ordinary reports can contain split records or shifted fields.
Easy local repair.
